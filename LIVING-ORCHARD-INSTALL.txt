HOW TO INSTALL:
1. Back up your current public/style.css.
2. From this ZIP copy public/style.css and public/bcc-falling-leaves.svg into your running ExamPortal/public folder.
3. Keep the existing bcc-campus-background.jpg and bcc-orchard-waves.svg in that folder.
4. Visit http://localhost:3000 and press Ctrl + F5.

If the leaf drift is too active, in the last CSS block change opacity:.52 to .25 or duration 36s to 55s.
