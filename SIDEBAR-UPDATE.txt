ExamPortal - Apple Green Sidebar Position Update

The sidebar now groups Overview, Available Exams, My Results, My Classes,
and Profile with even vertical spacing. Sign Out is pushed down near the
bottom, above the decorative landscape. The top-bar student profile is
unchanged; no routes, exam logic, database schema, or backend files changed.

Use your working .env file, start the project with npm start, and hard-refresh
Chrome (Ctrl+Shift+R). On screens 750px wide or narrower the original mobile
navigation remains in effect.
