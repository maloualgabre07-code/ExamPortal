ExamPortal - Chrome-compatible examination-only deterrents

Added to public/app.js and public/style.css:
- Visible exam-only integrity reminder and per-attempt identity watermark
- Restrict right-click, copy and cut inside the active exam content
- Intercept common Ctrl/Cmd+P, S and U shortcuts while exam is active
- Show a local count/reminder for tab/window focus loss
- Automatically disable deterrents when the exam is submitted, left, or signed out

LIMITATIONS:
Chrome cannot prevent OS screenshot keys, Snipping Tool, screen recording, camera photos, or all printing/shortcut workarounds. The focus-loss count is NOT persisted to PostgreSQL and is NOT an instructor audit trail. There are no database changes.

INSTALL:
Back up your existing ExamPortal folder. Copy the updated public/app.js and public/style.css into your project; preserve your own .env. Restart npm start and refresh the page (Ctrl+F5). Test student start/resume, navigating away, submit and instructor question editor.
